## Positioning statement

**Mimo answers one question: what can you actually prove about your AI agent's authority and behavior?**

Permission lists and risk scores blur five different things. Mimo keeps them apart and ties each to a record: what a person authorized, what the agent's tools permit, what it attempted, what it actually completed, and what nobody can say yet. When the evidence stops, Mimo says "unknown" instead of guessing.

Mimo works from the evidence you already have (task records, tool policies, traces). It does not monitor agents live or block actions today; enforcement appears in the demo only as a labeled concept.
