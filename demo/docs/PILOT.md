# Mimo Authority Gap Review · Founding Design Partner Pilot

*Proposal. Mimo is a provisional name. Powered by BoundaryProof.*

**For** teams deploying AI agents with connected tools.

**Objective.** Find where assigned agent tasks, configured tool permissions, and available execution evidence diverge.

**Scope**
- One selected agent workflow
- An agreed tool and permission inventory
- Up to 10 synthetic or sanitized test scenarios
- Evidence-based findings and a 30-minute readout

**Deliverables**
- Authority gap map (intended, configured, observed across 11 authority verbs)
- Prioritized findings, each tied to evidence identifiers
- Evidence and unknowns register (gaps are reported as gaps)
- Remediation recommendations
- Short executive summary

**Duration.** 5 business days after access and scope approval. Proposed, not a committed SLA.

**Commercial terms.** Pricing by proposal after scoping. No production access required for initial discovery. A paid pilot is the goal; nothing here implies one is sold.

**Data handling.** Start with synthetic data. Any real customer artifacts require written authorization, least-privilege access, agreed retention and deletion, and security review. No compliance certification is claimed.

**What it is not.** Not runtime enforcement, monitoring, a scanner, or an IAM replacement. The engine reasons over described and recorded agent behavior; it does not probe live agents. Enforcement appears in the demo only as a labeled concept simulation.

**Positioning.** Mimo answers: what can you actually prove about your agent's authority and behavior? It separates authority, capability, attempts, completed actions and open questions, each tied to a record.
